The Concept

Where the fairway meets the lantern. The International Club is a private members-only experience unlike anything else — nine holes of Lazer Golf surrounded by one of the most striking dining rooms on earth.

Inspired by the wabi-sabi philosophy of finding beauty in imperfection, every location pairs brutalist concrete architecture with the warmth of Japanese design — bonsai, fire channels, hand-thrown ceramics, and the deep red glow of paper lanterns suspended above it all.

This is not a golf club with a restaurant. This is a destination that happens to have a course.

Laser Golf

Precision-guided laser technology marks each hole's pin location from up to 400 yards — a beam of brilliant green that cuts through the night air and illuminates the mist above the green. Golf as spectacle.

Stadium lights flood the course after sundown. Play continues. The course never closes.
